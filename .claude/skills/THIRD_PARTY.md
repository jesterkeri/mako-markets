HyperFrames (https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes, Apache-2.0) is not vendored here.
It is enabled as a plugin in `.claude/settings.json` and installs from GitHub.

To update a vendored skill, re-copy its folder from the upstream repo and bump the commit above.
